I think this visualization is creative and attractive. I'm interested in wildlife and nature, and the topic of this visualization - The deadliest animals - attracted me.

## Step two: the critique
Advantages:
1. Used icons, labels, and captions to help understanding.
2. Visually appealing and attractive, including the use of colors, the layout, and the icons of animals.
3. Added the value of the number of humans killed each year below the animal names, which helps grab data faster.
4. The title is direct and clear.

Need to be optimized:
1. There is no explanation about why the block of humans is blue, different from other animals.
2. The scale. For animals with lower death counts it is difficult to see the specific differences from the visualization.
3. The text size of the note is small, which is difficult to read, and would make audience feel stressful.
